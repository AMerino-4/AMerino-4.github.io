About Me

--------------------------------

I am a sophomore at Wellesley College majoring in Computer Science. This portfolio showcases the UX/UI skills I have developed this semester in Human-Computer Interaction, alongside my broader Computer Science projects. I hope this display reflects my growth as a designer and developer, as well as the unique perspective I offer in creating student-centered solutions. My intended users for this portfolio are recruiters and my professors, so they can evaluate my technical skills and design thinking.

My Design Decisions:

Color Scheme: My palette consists of a range of dark to light purples, earthy greens, and soft whites. I chose these colors to give my website a fun, accessible, and sophisticated vibe. While purple is one of my favorite colors, the soft background also serves a functional purpose by ensuring high readability and reducing eye strain for users.

Typography: I chose Plus Jakarta Sans to maximize readability. It is a versatile font that remains clear and legible whether it is set to bold or normal weights, making it perfect for a digital portfolio.

Layout:I utilized a grid-based system throughout my pages to prioritize the Gestalt Principle of Common Region. This organization allows the content to "pop" against the purple background, making the sections look intentional, organized, and professional.

Images: I included my professional headshot to establish a personal connection and portray a professional image. I also incorporated logos from my previous Computer Science projects to provide visual context for my technical background.

Modifications: I modified the original template by adding custom grid layouts. On the index page, I moved the "Projects" and "Resume" buttons to the bottom to ensure the "About Me" section remains the primary focus. For the Projects page, I implemented a 2x2 grid inspired by the many UX/UI portfolios I researched online. To build this, I used Tailwind CSS to customize the layouts. While I was initially unfamiliar with Tailwind, my previous experience in a summer HTML/CSS program (and my notes) helped me quickly relearn HTML/CSS and be more creative with the design!
